# Visual Style Guide

## Core look
Dark cinematic pixel art with atmospheric lighting, rich shadows, restrained highlights, and carefully composed 9:16 frames.

## Palette
- Midnight navy and charcoal for night environments
- Muted teal and cold blue for rain and reflections
- Warm amber for café windows, lamps, and practical lights
- Small accents of muted red for visual contrast

## Composition
- Vertical 9:16
- Clear focal subject in the middle third
- Foreground, midground, and background separated for parallax
- Leave clean negative space for captions
- Avoid tiny unreadable details

## Motion language
Use slow camera pushes, gentle parallax, falling rain, steam, flickering lights, passing reflections, and subtle character movement. Avoid frantic motion unless the story requires it.

## Continuity checklist
- Same character proportions and outfit in every shot
- Consistent time of day and weather
- Matching light direction and palette
- Props stay in the same hand or position between connected shots
- No accidental text, logos, or watermarks
