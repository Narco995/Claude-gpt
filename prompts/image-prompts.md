# Image Prompt Templates

## Environment master prompt
A richly detailed vertical 9:16 dark cinematic pixel-art scene of [LOCATION] at [TIME], [WEATHER]. Deep navy shadows, muted teal reflections, warm amber practical lights, layered foreground and background, atmospheric depth, crisp intentional pixel clusters, cinematic composition, no text, no logo, no watermark.

## Character consistency prompt
The same recurring character: [CHARACTER DESCRIPTION]. Preserve exact hairstyle, face shape, clothing, colors, body proportions, and accessories. Place the character [ACTION] in [LOCATION]. Match the established dark cinematic pixel-art style, lighting direction, and palette. Vertical 9:16, no text, no logo, no watermark.

## Layer extraction prompt
Create the same scene as a clean background plate with the main character and movable foreground objects excluded. Preserve camera angle, lighting, perspective, and all environmental details. Vertical 9:16, dark cinematic pixel art.

## Negative guidance
Do not change character identity, outfit, camera angle, architectural layout, or weather continuity. Avoid photorealism, 3D rendering, blurry pixel edges, extra limbs, distorted hands, text, subtitles, logos, and watermarks.
